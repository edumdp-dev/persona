Interview answers

22 questions · Original worksheet numbering · English answers to memorize

1. Can you tell me a little about yourself and describe your background in brief?

My name is Lucas Costa, I'm 24 and I graduated in Business Administration at FEA-USP. I did a one-and-a-half-year internship at Itaú in customer service operations. I love solving customer problems using data.

Name → 24 → FEA-USP → Itaú → data

2. How did you hear about the position?

I saw the job post on PicPay's LinkedIn page. A friend who works here also told me the team is great, so I applied right away.

LinkedIn + friend

3. What do you know about our company?

PicPay started in 2012 in Vitória as a payment app and today it's one of the biggest digital banks in Brazil, with more than 60 million users. You offer Pix, credit cards, loans and investments, all in one app.

2012 → Vitória → 60 million → all in one app

4. Why do you want this job?

Because I want to help millions of people have a simple and good experience with their money. PicPay is innovative and customer-focused, and that matches what I believe in.

Millions of people → simple → innovative

5. What is your 'dream' job?

My dream job is to lead a Customer Experience team at a big tech company. I want to make products easier for everyone to use.

Lead CX team → big tech → easier

6. What is leadership in your opinion?

For me, leadership is giving the example and helping people grow. A good leader listens, gives clear goals and takes responsibility when things go wrong.

Example → grow → listen, goals, responsibility

7. Why should we hire you? What makes you a good fit for this position?

I already have experience in customer service at a big bank, and I know how to use data to improve it. I learn fast, I'm organized and I really care about customers.

Experience → data → fast, organized, care

8. What are your greatest professional strengths?

My greatest strengths are organization and communication. I can explain complex problems in a simple way, and I always deliver on time.

Organization + communication → on time

9. What do you consider your weaknesses?

Sometimes I'm a perfectionist and spend too much time on details. Now I set deadlines for each task, and that helps me a lot.

Perfectionist → deadlines

10. How do you deal with pressure or stressful situations?

I stay calm and make a list of priorities. I solve the most urgent things first, and I talk to my team if I need help.

Calm → priorities → urgent first → team

11. What is your greatest professional achievement?

At Itaú, I created an Excel dashboard to track customer complaints. It helped my team reduce response time by 20%.

Itaú → Excel dashboard → 20% faster

12. Tell me about a challenge or conflict you've faced at work, and how you dealt with it.

Once, two colleagues disagreed about how to answer a difficult client. I organized a quick meeting, we listened to both ideas and combined them. The client was happy and the team got closer.

2 colleagues → meeting → combine → happy client

13. Where do you see yourself in five years?

In five years, I see myself as a senior analyst or coordinator here at PicPay. I want to lead projects that improve the customer experience.

Senior / coordinator → lead projects

14. What are you looking for in a new position?

I'm looking for a place where I can learn every day and grow my career. I also want a young team and real challenges.

Learn → grow → young team → challenges

15. How would your boss and co-workers describe you?

They would say I'm reliable, friendly and always ready to help. My boss used to say I was the most organized person on the team.

Reliable, friendly, helpful → most organized

16. How do you deal with pressure or stressful situations?

(same question as #10 — same answer)

I stay calm and make a list of priorities. I solve the most urgent things first, and I talk to my team if I need help.

Same as #10: Calm → priorities → urgent first → team

17. What would you do in your first 90 days in this position?

First, I would learn about the products, the team and the main customer problems. Then, I would look at the data and suggest one quick improvement.

Learn → data → one quick improvement

18. What do you like to do outside of work?

I love playing soccer with my friends on the weekends and going running. I also like cooking for my family.

Soccer → running → cooking

19. If you were an animal, which animal would you want to be? Why?

I would be a dolphin, because dolphins are smart, friendly and always work in groups. Just like me in a team!

Dolphin → smart, friendly, groups

20. Briefly, tell us something about your previous working experiences.

I was an intern at Itaú for one and a half years, in customer service operations. I analyzed complaints, created reports and helped improve our processes.

Itaú 1.5 years → complaints, reports, processes

21. Flexibility and Salary. How important are they in your opinion?

Both are important, but for me learning and growth come first. A fair salary and some flexibility, like a hybrid model, help me be more productive.

Both important → growth first → fair + hybrid

22. Do you have any questions for us?

Yes! What does a typical day look like for this team? And what are the next steps in the process?

Typical day? → Next steps?
